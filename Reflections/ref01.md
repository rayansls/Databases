# Reflection 1 in Module 1

- What is your take-away, what do you want to remember, what would be your advice?
  Take-away can be small summary, but better outstanding points.

The main take-away from this first session is that this course is more about understanding the workflow than about the technical tasks themselves. Setting up the repository and the toolchain was not hard; the real challenge was figuring out what the professor actually expected from us, because the amount of information and instructions was overwhelming, especially since most of us are not used to working with GitHub.
- What was good?

The two breaks. Other modules usually give only one, but here the material was heavy and partly confusing.
- What was difficult, where did you struggle?
The struggle was the fact that there was a lot of information, and it was not always easy to follow and understand what the professor wanted us to do.
